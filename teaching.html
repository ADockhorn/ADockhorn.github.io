Game Development & Learning Technologies at SDU

I teach in the Game Development & Learning Technologies (GDLT) program at the SDU Metaverse Lab, University of Southern Denmark. The program combines interactive media, software engineering, and applied AI, training students to design, build, and study modern interactive systems.

My courses sit at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and game development — covering decision-making algorithms, search and planning, reinforcement learning, and the practical use of AI inside game engines. I also co-supervise BSc and MSc theses on AI for games, agent-based modelling, and data-driven decision support.
